# Apple App Store — English

**Name:** Liftria: Food & Workout Log

**Subtitle:** Meals, workouts & progress

**Promotional text:** One place for food, strength workouts, weight trends and water. Your logs stay on your phone. Optional Hiku meal estimates need your own Anthropic API key.

**Description:**

Build routines you can actually follow.

Liftria combines a food diary, strength-training log and progress tracking in a simple, local-first experience. No Liftria account or cloud database required.

FOOD, WITHOUT THE GUESSWORK
Log meals and portions, including Egyptian staples. See calories, macros, fiber, sugar and sodium when those values are available. Missing nutrients remain unknown, not zero. Count glasses of water with a quick tap.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN
Organize exercises and workout templates. Record sets, reps, loads and rest, and revisit your training history. Get optional warm-up and plate-loading suggestions when you need them.

SEE THE BIGGER PICTURE
Track body weight and view trends over time. With enough logged food days and weigh-ins, Liftria estimates energy expenditure from your history and can suggest a new weekly calorie target—never changing it without your approval. Review supported device health data where the integration is available and you allow access.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
Your diary, training, weight and preferences live on your phone. Export backups and CSV files on demand. No Liftria account, ads or automatic cloud sync.

HIKU IS OPTIONAL
Bring your own Anthropic API key to request AI meal estimates from text or a photo (photo mode is Beta). Requests use the internet, and content you choose to send is processed by Anthropic. You review every estimate before it becomes a diary entry.

Liftria is for personal tracking; it does not provide medical advice.
