# Google Play — English

**App name:** Liftria: Food & Workout Log

**Short description:** Track meals, workouts and progress—privately, on your phone.

**Full description:**

Food, training and progress belong together. Liftria brings your daily logs into one calm, practical place—without requiring an account or a cloud database.

MAKE EVERY DAY COUNT

• Log meals and portions, including familiar Egyptian foods. Review calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium when their sources provide them.
• Track strength workouts, sets, reps, weights and rest. Build plans, use warm-up and plate suggestions, and review your session history.
• Record your weight and follow its trend. When you have enough food and weigh-in history, Liftria can estimate your daily energy expenditure and propose a weekly calorie-target update. You decide whether to accept it.
• Keep a simple water-glass count alongside your food diary.
• Inspect compatible device health records where supported and where you grant permission. Available integrations differ between Android and iPhone.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

Your profile, diary, training, weight and settings are stored locally on your phone. No Liftria account, ads, cloud synchronization or analytics account is needed. Export a backup or CSV files whenever you choose.

OPTIONAL HIKU AI

Describe a meal or try photo estimates (Beta) to get a starting point for your food log. Hiku requires your own Anthropic API key and an internet connection. Only when you send a request is its selected text or photo and relevant context sent to Anthropic for processing. Review and edit estimates before saving; photo estimates may be wrong.

Missing food nutrients stay unknown rather than appearing as zero. Health-derived totals are not guessed. Liftria is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device or a source of medical advice.

Start with one entry, build a routine and see your progress over time.
